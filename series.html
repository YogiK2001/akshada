— six series, one silence —

The work, arranged by name.

Each series is a long conversation with a single form — entered, lived with, and left only when it asks to be left.

01 · shiva · meditation

Adiyogi

The first one to sit still. These canvases began as a question — what is left of a face when the seeking ends? — and slowly became a study of stillness in deep blue.

Fourteen works to date, painted in winter mornings between 2024 and 2026. Pigment ground by hand, surface burnished to a soft sheen.

02 · the mother

Devi

She is seated. She is not waiting. Studies of the feminine ground — sindoor red on rough cotton, the gold leaf raised by a single fingertip.

Made in tribute to the small village shrines of the Sahyadris, where she has been quietly held for a very long time.

see the works →

03 · mountain & moon

Shiva–Shakti

Two are one. Paired canvases meant to be hung together — Kailash and Manasarovar, the still and the moving — though each can also stand alone.

see the works →

04 · the watcher

Naga

Coiled, patient, never asleep. Small format works on hand-prepared boards — the serpent as guardian of the inner threshold, painted in burnt sienna and old gold.

see the works →

05 · the third sight

Sahasrara

The thousand-petalled lotus, abstracted to almost nothing. The newest series, still finding its voice. Soft violets, beaten silver leaf, a single quiet point of gold.

see the works →

06 · lotus on water

Manasarovar

A long study of stillness on the surface of a lake the artist has never seen, but has often dreamt. The lotus is small in every canvas. The water is not.
