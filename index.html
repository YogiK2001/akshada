About the artist

Akshada paints Adiyogi, Devi, the Naga, the linga, Hanuman and Ganapati in acrylic, in a studio in Pune.

She works slowly, treating each painting as a practice of sitting with a form until it settles. Much of the work is waiting, and a good deal of it is leaving things out.
