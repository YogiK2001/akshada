Notes, slowly written.

Short entries on the work as it happens — pigment, the weather, what the canvas refuses, and what it agrees to in the end.

on shiva · 12 jan 2026

Why I painted Adiyogi with no face

The features kept arriving — a brow, the suggestion of a nose, the curve of a closed eye — and I kept wiping them off with the side of my palm. By the seventh morning I understood I had not been failing to finish him. I had been finishing him each time.

The face that finally stayed is no face. A dark oval, a thin band of gold above where a third eye would be if there were eyes. It is the most honest portrait I have made.

process · 04 jan 2026

Grinding pigment on a cold morning

Lapis from a small dealer in Jaipur, sindoor from the temple lane near my mother's house, and a brass mortar that is older than I am — older than my mother. There is a particular blue you can only get by hand, by giving it the full forty minutes it asks for.

When the kettle whistles I am usually a third of the way through. By the time the tea has gone cold the colour is ready.

studio · 28 dec 2025

The lotus does not pose for me

A small note on patience, water, and the lake near my mother's village in winter. I have been trying to paint that lotus for two seasons. It opens at a different hour each time I arrive. I am beginning to suspect this is the lesson.

on shiva · 14 dec 2025

Kailash, in a small room in Pune

I have never seen the mountain. I keep painting it anyway. A friend who has been three times says my version is wrong in every detail and right in the only one that matters. I am taking the compliment.

studio · 02 dec 2025

On leaving things unfinished

Half the canvases in the studio are not finished. Some never will be. I used to feel guilty about this. Now I think of them as long sittings the work and I are still having together.

process · 19 nov 2025

A small fire, every evening

I light a small fire on the studio veranda before I begin. It is not ceremony exactly. It is more like the way one might warm a stranger's hands before asking them in.
