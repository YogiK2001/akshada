— commissioned works —

A canvas, for your room.

The studio accepts a small number of private commissions each year — usually four. Each begins with a conversation, and is paced to the work, not the calendar.

If you would like a piece made for your home, your shrine, or someone you love, please write. The studio will respond personally — never with a quote and a timeline, but with a question or two, and a sense of whether the work is one I can honestly make.

Below is the rhythm a commission usually follows.

i

A letter

You write to the studio with what is in your mind — a form, a feeling, a wall, a memory. Detail helps but is not required. Sometimes a single sentence is enough.

ii

A conversation

If the work feels right for the studio, we speak — by call or by letter. We agree on size, palette, and the long, soft window in which the painting will be made.

iii

A first study

A small painted study is sent to you, with notes. Adjustments are welcome at this stage and at no other. After this, the work and I are alone for a while.

iv

The sitting

The canvas is painted in the studio, slowly, in the morning hours. It is not photographed in progress. You will hear from me when it is finished.

v

Delivery

The work is varnished, blessed, and shipped wrapped in cotton. A handwritten note accompanies every painting.

Write to the studio

Replies usually within a week. The studio is small — please bear with us.

Your letter is on its way. The studio will reply soon.
