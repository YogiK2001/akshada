About Akshada

Akshada paints Adiyogi, Devi, the Naga, the linga, Hanuman and Ganapati in acrylic, in a studio in Pune.

The painter

Each subject is less a subject than a presence she returns to.

She works slowly, in acrylic, treating each painting as a practice of sitting with a form until it settles. Much of the work is waiting, and a good deal of it is leaving things out.

The paintings are made for quiet rooms: a wall in a home, a corner for sitting, a place where the eye can rest and the mind can follow.

How the paintings are made

The paintings are made in acrylic, on canvas and on paper. Deep blues and reds are built in thin layers, each left to settle before the next, until the colour has depth rather than surface.

Gold appears sparingly, as an accent: a moon, a thread of light. Most of the painting happens in the quiet morning hours. Every original is one of a kind.

The studio in Pune

The studio is in Pune, India. It can be visited by appointment: please write first, and a time will be found together.
